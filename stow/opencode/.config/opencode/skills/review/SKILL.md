# Review

First load the `planner` skill (which itself loads `notetaking`).

A retrospective, not a planning session — this looks backward at work
already completed, rather than forward at what to do next (that's
`daily-planning`). Can happen at whatever cadence makes sense: after a
single piece of work, at the end of a day, a week, or whenever the user
wants to reflect. Not locked to "evening" or "daily."

## What this covers

- What worked, in the work being reviewed.
- What didn't work, or what got in the way.
- What to do differently next time — a concrete adjustment, not just a
  vague intention.

## Behavior

- Ask what they want to review if it's not already clear (a specific
  task, today's work generally, the past week, etc.).
- Let them talk through it — this is closer to a brainstorming
  conversation than a guided worksheet. Don't impose `daily-planning`'s
  fixed step sequence here.
- Capture the outcome as a note in `planner.org`'s Journal section, under
  today's dated entry (create one if it doesn't exist yet, per the
  `planner` skill's conventions), rather than a separate file.
- If the review surfaces a new goal, action item, or something to
  schedule, offer to hand that off appropriately (a new Backlog TODO, a
  new habit under Daily/Weekly/Monthly, etc.) rather than leaving it
  buried in prose.
